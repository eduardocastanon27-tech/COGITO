## Business Models — What Actually Works

### Digital Products (Best margin structure)
- 70–85% margins when it works
- Etsy auto-suspends AI-spam accounts (50+ near-identical listings/day)
- Working: journals, workbooks, business templates, kids content, niche design bundles
- Gothic botanical, vintage sci-fi, boho wedding — aesthetic niches too small for big players

### Print-on-Demand (Niche-dependent)
- Generic T-shirts: dead. Niche products: 30-35% margins on home decor/canvas
- Working approach: reverse-engineer bestsellers → themed bundles ($15-50 large collections)
- Sellers going from $568/month → $1,500-2,100/month by niche specialization
- Etsy requires AI disclosure labeling as of June 2025

### Dropshipping (Works with fast suppliers)
- Classic AliExpress model dead. Domestic (3-7 day ship) + niche branding = 15-25% margins
- $2K-10K/month achievable but takes 6-12 months

### TikTok Shop
- Avg active seller: ~$690/month profit, 18.4% margin
- Organic content sellers: 35-39% margins
- Platform fees: 6% referral + ~2% processing + 5-30% creator commission
- Only works if content creation is a core activity, not passive listing

### Reselling / Arbitrage
- eBay secondhand growing fast; thrift flipping still viable
- Hard ceiling: physical presence required, not scalable solo

## AI Edge Points (Real vs Hype)

**Real edges:**
- Micro-niche speed: AI lets 1 person serve 50 sub-niches simultaneously ("cases for ICU travel nurses")
- Trend detection 48h before mass market: Glimpse, Sell The Trend, EchoTik watch Reddit/Discord/TikTok acceleration signals
- AI photo enhancement (NOT replacement): AI-enhanced real photos convert 2.4x better than fully synthetic
- Review responses: 30 seconds vs 5 minutes manually, no extra cost beyond ChatGPT

**Dead:**
- Fully AI-generated product hero images: 73% of consumers less confident; 22% of returns attributed to misleading AI photos
- AI design dumps: "generate and dump" is dead; curation + aesthetic coherence matters

## Low-Competition Niches (Under $100 entry)

1. Hyper-specific phone cases: "cases for travel nurses," "ADHD creator cases" — 250-500% markup
2. Gothic botanical / vintage sci-fi digital bundle packs — aesthetic specificity blocks mass AI dumpers
3. Pet portrait digital downloads — emotional purchase, high intent, 65%+ margin
4. Wellness stationery for specific communities (neurodivergent, recovery, ADHD) — underserved by generic wellness
5. Functional trending home decor (mushroom lamps, etc.) on TikTok Shop — still in acceleration phase

**Strategic rule:** Enter a proven broad category (wall art, phone cases, stickers), then be far more specific than incumbents. 3-5 rapid-launch designs beats month-long research — only real sales signal matters.
